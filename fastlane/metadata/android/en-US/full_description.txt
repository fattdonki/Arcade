<h1>Arcade</h1>
<p>Arcade is a minimal, Material You-themed game launcher. It allows you to block offline games from accessing the internet using Shizuku or a VPN. This saves battery, blocks ads, and protects your privacy.</p>
<h2>Features</h2>
<ul>
<li>Distraction free launcher letting you focus on your library, with clean Material design</li>
<li>Block selected games from accessing the network</li>
<li>Rename games</li>
<li>Automatically enable or disable Do Not Disturb for selected games</li>
<li>Organize your library by sorting games into groups</li>
</ul>
